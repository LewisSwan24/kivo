Kivo

Kivo is the upload component of a student team's content tool, built for a university course project. When a team member uploads a video in the tool, Kivo stores it in a Google Drive folder that the team uses for the project.

Kivo only asks for access to the Drive files it creates itself. It cannot open, list or change any other file in the Google account that connects it.

It is not a public service and has no sign-up.
